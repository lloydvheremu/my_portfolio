Chess, Philosophy & Systematic Thinking

Software engineering is how I earn a living, but systems thinking is how I experience the world.

I'm an introvert who enjoys deep, uninterrupted focus. When I'm away from editor windows and deployment logs, you'll usually find me analyzing a chess position, solving a complex sudoku, or with my nose in a book.

To me, chess is not just a game — it's a mirror of software architecture. In chess (especially variants like Chess960, which removes memorized openings and tests pure pattern evaluation), you can't make a move without considering every pawn structure trade-off. In enterprise ERP, a database schema change or fiscal validation rule requires the exact same foresight.
